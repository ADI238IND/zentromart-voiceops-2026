# Demo included in this submission

`demo.mp4` shows actual API-generated customer speech, Gnani transcription, the shared support agent, Gnani spoken responses and source/tool traces. Both voices are synthetic Gnani voices; that is disclosed on screen and in narration. No voice cloning or third-party speech provider is used.

The three turns cover the electronics return window, the pre-August-2026 exception as a follow-up, and successful creation of a mock human-handover ticket. All agent replies in the video come from the running agent, without hand editing. Introductory and closing narration explain the architecture and limits. This is a visual walkthrough of actual saved turns, not a live phone integration.

Actual evidence records and generated reply audio are under `sample_outputs/audio/demo_session/`. Each customer clip is under 60 seconds. The whole video is under the brief's two-minute limit.
